What DEMEOS Understands

DEMEOS uses trusted evidence from your business activity to improve the context behind future recommendations.

Different evidence types remain separate so Customer Feedback, customer participation, owner-recorded outcomes and owner decisions are not mistaken for one another. Feedback shows possibility relevance only, and Interested signals show interest only — neither is a sale, conversion, revenue, booking or proof of success.
